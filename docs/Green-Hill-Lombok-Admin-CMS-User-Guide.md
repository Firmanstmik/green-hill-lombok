# Welcome

The Green Hill Admin is where you look after the website yourself: no code, no developer in between. Everything a visitor sees on the website is edited here, and everything visitors send you arrives here.

With it you can:

- **Edit every page of the website**: the homepage, About / Reece, Why Lombok, Buying in Lombok, Green Hill Private, the Opportunities and Notes pages, the enquiry page, and the footer.
- **Present opportunities**: add land and investment opportunities, set their price and status, feature them on the homepage, keep them private, or present them as a Green Hill Private teaser.
- **Publish Notes**: short field notes and articles for the journal.
- **Follow every enquiry**: see who reached out, what they are looking for, and where each conversation stands.
- **Look after search and sharing**: how pages appear on Google and when a link is shared on WhatsApp or social media.
- **Manage who can sign in**: add or remove admins.

> Nothing you change reaches the website until you publish it. Drafts, previews and notes are only ever visible to you.

A few words you will see throughout:

- **Draft**: saved, but not on the website yet.
- **Publish**: put it on the website now.
- **Preview**: see the real page with your changes before anyone else does.
- **Original copy**: the approved wording the website launched with. You can always return to it.

# 01 · Signing in

The admin lives at the website address followed by **/en/admin**. Until the Green Hill domain is connected that is `green-hill-lombok.vercel.app/en/admin`; afterwards it is the same path on the new address. Bookmark it.

1. Open the admin address in your browser.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your password. The eye button shows what you typed, if you want to check it.
4. Click **Sign in**.

::: figure d-01-login wide
The sign-in page.
1. Your email address.
2. Your password. The eye shows or hides it.
3. Sign in.
4. Forgot password? Sends you a link to choose a new one.
:::

**Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot password?**, enter your email address and click **Send reset link**. An email arrives with a link; open it and choose a new password (at least 12 characters, with a capital letter, a small letter and a number). The link works once. If it has expired, simply ask for a new one.

::: figure d-02-forgot half
Asking for a new password.
1. The email address you sign in with.
2. Send reset link.
:::

You stay signed in on your own computer and phone until you sign out. To sign out, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Sign out**. Nobody can open any admin page without signing in, and there is no public sign-up: only people you add as admins can get in.

::: figure m-01-login phone

## Editing a field

1. Open the page and find the section (use the section list at the top).
2. Click into a field and type. Each field has a plain label and, where useful, a short hint underneath.
3. Stop typing. After a moment the status under the page title changes to **Draft saved**. Your change is safe, but not public yet.
4. Check the live preview on the right, or click **Preview**.
5. When you are happy, click **Publish**.

::: figure d-14-field-edit wide
Editing a field.
1. The field you changed.
2. Restore original: puts back the approved wording for this one field.
3. The save status: Draft saved, and Draft · not yet published beneath the title.
4. Discard changes: throws away the whole draft and returns to what is on the website now.
:::

> A vertical bar | in a headline starts a new line, exactly where the design breaks it. Leave the bars in place unless you want the line breaks to move.

**Photographs.** Click **Upload a new image** under a photograph to replace it. Always fill in the **Image description**: one plain sentence saying what the photograph shows. It is read aloud to people who cannot see the image and helps search engines. Photographs marked *Original image* are the ones the site launched with.

**Other languages.** Switch the language tabs above the sections to edit the Indonesian, Dutch or Spanish version. When you change the English wording, the other languages show **Needs translation** on that field until you update them; until then visitors in those languages keep seeing the older, approved translation.

## Opportunity statuses

- **Draft**: being prepared. Never on the website.
- **Available**: live on the website and open to enquiries.
- **Reserved**: still shown, marked Reserved, while a sale is being agreed.
- **Sold**: still shown, marked Sold. Useful as a record of what Green Hill has placed.
- **Archived**: taken off the website and out of your everyday lists. Nothing is deleted; **Restore as draft** brings it back.

Separately from the status, each opportunity is either **Public** or **Private**:

- **Private** opportunities are handled directly through Green Hill and are never listed on the website. They appear in your **Opportunities › Private** workspace.
- A private opportunity can be presented as a **Green Hill Private teaser** (below).

Choose the status that is true today, and change it as soon as it changes: visitors trust what they read.

## Featured opportunities

**Featured** opportunities are the ones that may appear in the homepage's *Selected opportunities* section. The number shown on the homepage is set in **Content › Homepage › 03 Selected opportunities**.

- **To feature**: open the ••• menu on the opportunity and choose **Feature on the homepage**, or switch on **Featured** in Step 1.
- **To stop featuring**: choose **Remove from featured**, or switch it off in Step 1.
- Use **Featured only** on the opportunities list to see which are featured.
- Private opportunities cannot be featured.

# 08 · SEO & social sharing

Every page, opportunity and note has a **Search & sharing** section. Everything in it is optional: when a field is empty, the admin uses a sensible default (the title, the summary, the primary photograph).

- **SEO title** (*Title on Google*): the blue headline in a Google result. Around 60 characters show in full.
- **Meta description** (*Description on Google*): the two lines of text under it. Around 155 characters show in full. Write it for a person deciding whether to click.
- **Public page address**: the end of the web address, for example `/property/selong-belanak-hillside-land`. It is fixed once the page is published, so links people have shared keep working.
- **Canonical link** (*Original page address*): only for something first published on another website. Usually leave it empty.
- **Social image** (*Sharing image*): the picture shown when the link is shared on WhatsApp, email or social media.

The website-wide defaults are in **Settings › SEO & social**.

::: figure d-61-seo-settings wide
Settings › SEO & social.
1. Site title: the website's name, used where a page has no title of its own.
2. Site description: used where a page has no description of its own.
3. Each page: a list of every page and whether it has its own search wording.
:::

# 11 · Publishing

Every change follows the same calm path:

**Draft → Preview → Review → Publish**

- **Draft**: the admin saves your work as you go. Only you see it.
- **Preview**: see exactly what visitors will see, on desktop and on a phone.
- **Review**: read it once more. For opportunities, the Review step lists anything missing.
- **Publish**: it goes live straight away.

> Only publish when the content has been checked. Visitors, search engines and anyone you have shared a link with see the published version immediately.

If you publish something by mistake: for a page, change it back (**Restore original** puts back the approved wording) and publish again; for an opportunity or note, choose **Unpublish**.

# 12 · Common tasks

**Add a new property**
1. Opportunities › **Add opportunity**.
2. Basics: title, type, summary, Public, price → **Save draft**.
3. Location: region and area.
4. Land & specifications: land size, tenure, zoning.
5. Media: four to six photographs, each with a description.
6. Story: description and *Why Green Hill likes it*.
7. Review → **Preview the memo** → choose *Available* → **Publish opportunity**.

**Change a property's price**
Open the opportunity → Basics → change the price → **Save changes** (if it is live) → check with **Preview**.

**Mark a property Reserved**
Opportunities → ••• on the property → **Mark as reserved** → confirm. (Or Basics → Status → Reserved → **Save changes**.)

**Archive a property**
••• on the property → **Archive opportunity** → **Archive**. It leaves the website at once. To bring it back: Archived → ••• → **Restore as draft**.

**Replace a property photo**
Open the opportunity → Media → **Replace** under the photograph → choose the new file → update its description → **Save changes**.

**Feature a property**
••• on the property → **Feature on the homepage**. To stop: **Remove from featured**.

# 14 · Troubleshooting

**"I edited something but don't see it on the website."**
It is probably still a draft. Open it and look beneath the title: *Draft · not yet published* means you still need to click **Publish** (or **Save changes** for a live opportunity or note). Then refresh the website page.

**"The image upload is still loading."**
Large photographs are resized before upload, which can take a few seconds on a slow connection. Keep the page open. If it does not finish, try again with a smaller file (under 5 MB, JPEG, PNG or WebP).

**"The preview looks different."**
The preview shows your saved draft, so wait for *Draft saved* and reopen the preview. On phones the homepage uses *Headline on phones* when it is filled in. Other languages show their own translation.

**"I cannot publish."**
Something required is missing. The page shows *Before publishing* with the list, and for opportunities the Review step has an **Add** button next to each missing item. Common reasons: a summary shorter than 20 characters, no primary photograph, an email address or WhatsApp number that is not complete.

**"I forgot my password."**
On the sign-in page click **Forgot password?**, enter your email address and follow the link in the email. Another admin can also send you a reset link from Users & Admins.

**"A property is not appearing publicly."**
Check three things: it is published (Available, Reserved or Sold, not Draft or Archived); it is **Public** (Private opportunities are never listed); and, if you expected it on the homepage, that it is **Featured**.

**"It says Changed elsewhere."**
The same item was saved on another device or by another admin. Reload the page to see the latest version before continuing.
